*Breathing the Moon* demonstrates this principle in a minimal form. A respiratory sensor connects a participant’s breathing to the changing illumination of a rolling moon-model. It shared cyclical rhythm between breath and lunar change, inhaling and exhaling, brightening and dimming. Participants reported greater awareness of their breathing and found it helpful for breathing practice.
